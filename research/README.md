# Research

Survey reports and the sourced notes behind them, written while developing the
acceptance test in this repository.

- `reports/` - finished reports: existing RFC 2544 / Y.1564 tools for MikroTik,
  and hardware test options (smart SFPs, NIDs, TRex).
- `notes/` - the working notes each report was built from, with their sources.
  They were written for this project's maintainer, so "the user" and "the owner"
  usually mean the maintainer and this project's script and bench; in forum
  summaries "the user" can also mean the poster being quoted.
- `notes/btest-technical-manual-review/` - a review of an earlier internal btest
  write-up against the bench evidence in `results/`.

Raw inputs (search dumps, AI-tool output) are not published; the notes cite
the original sources directly.

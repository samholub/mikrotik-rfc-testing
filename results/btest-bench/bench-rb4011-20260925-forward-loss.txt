Forward loss at SLA resolution (fault F4): what one end can and cannot see
Date   : 2026-09-25
Path   : BENCH-NEW RB4011 (ROS 7.19.5) ether2 <-> ether2 BENCH-712 RB4011 (ROS 7.20.7), direct cable
Faults : raw prerouting drop, nth=N,1, on the RECEIVING router's ether2. Counts are that rule's counters.

--- E1: does any btest direction report forward loss in lost-packets? ---
512 B (setting 494), 20 s, connection-count=20, 18,385,712 bps
E1a direction=transmit, 10% dropped into BENCH-712 : dropped 9,323   lostsum 0      tx-avg 15,561,648 (-10%)
E1b direction=receive,  10% dropped into BENCH-NEW : dropped 9,310   lostsum 8,471  (91%)
Test 1 (fault-matrix record), direction=both        : forward drops absent, reverse 93% counted
=> lost-packets only ever counts loss INTO the router that started the test.
   A second leg from the same end (transmit or receive) cannot see forward loss.
